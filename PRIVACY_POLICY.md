# Privacy Policy for ! maja !

Effective date: 20 August 2026

## Information Processed

! maja ! processes Discord server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, user IDs, ticket lifecycle records, staff availability, moderation records, command activity, and diagnostic references required to provide its features.

Ticket message content, attachments, display names, and avatars may be processed when the bot creates a transcript, evaluates ticket inactivity, or performs enabled moderation functions.

## Purpose

Information is used only to configure the bot, operate tickets, enforce server-selected moderation actions, produce requested transcripts, calculate staff availability, diagnose failures, and protect the service from abuse.

## Storage and Retention

Each server's operational records are separated by its Discord server ID. Configuration, ticket records, moderation records, availability records, and generated transcripts are removed when the bot is removed from that server. Rotating operational logs may retain limited technical identifiers until normal log rotation completes.

Server administrators can clear active bot configuration with `/setup reset`. Existing Discord channels and Discord-hosted messages remain controlled by the server administrator.

## Sharing

Data is not sold. Information is disclosed only when required to operate the hosting service, comply with applicable law, protect users or the service, or respond to a valid request from the affected server administrator.

## Security

The service uses restricted bot permissions, per-server data isolation, protected credentials, and sanitized diagnostics. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## User Controls

Server administrators may remove the bot to trigger deletion of stored server data. Questions and deletion requests can be submitted through the support location configured on the bot's Discord application profile.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when features, hosting, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
